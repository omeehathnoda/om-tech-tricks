Deal check

Big discount labels can hide an inflated “original” price. Compare the current offer with the normal street price before deciding.

  • Check history. Compare against the usual price.
  • Match the model. Storage, RAM, generation and warranty matter.
  • Count the extras. Include delivery and required accessories.
