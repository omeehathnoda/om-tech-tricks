TRAVEL DATA

✅ iTechieSIM travel eSIM

Based in Multan, Pakistan, iTechieSIM sells prepaid travel data eSIMs for 195+ countries. The site shows a 7-day plan for Rs 2,215.

  1. Open itechiesim.com, then pick your country and data plan.
  2. Pay. You get a QR code by email and WhatsApp in about 30 seconds.
  3. On iPhone, go to Settings → Mobile Data → Add eSIM and scan the QR code. Turn on Data Roaming.
  4. Keep your normal SIM on for WhatsApp and bank OTPs. The eSIM gives you internet.

Honest note: This does not give a non-PTA phone a local Pakistani SIM. It only gives travel data through an eSIM.

WINDOWS

Open your clipboard history

Press Windows + V to retrieve text and images you copied earlier. Turn it on the first time the panel opens.

BROWSER

Reopen a closed tab

Press Ctrl + Shift + T on Windows or Cmd + Shift + T on Mac. Repeat it to restore more tabs.

ANDROID

Pin an app for a hand-off

Enable App Pinning in Security settings, then pin one app before handing over your phone. Other apps stay out of reach.

02 / HACKS

Simple fixes

4 notes
WI-FI

Wi-Fi feels slow?

Test beside the router first. If speed improves there, move the router higher and into the open before buying an extender.

STORAGE

Phone storage full?

Sort downloads and videos by size, remove offline media you can re-download, then empty the app’s own trash. Skip “cleaner” apps promising miracle speedups.

LAPTOP

Laptop running hot?

Check Task Manager or Activity Monitor for unusual CPU use. Restart that app, clear blocked vents and keep the laptop off soft bedding.

BATTERY

Battery draining overnight?

Review usage by app, disable unnecessary background activity and test one night with airplane mode to separate weak-signal drain from app drain.
