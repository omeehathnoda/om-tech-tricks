Privacy Policy — snapalerts

Telegram bot: @omtechwatchbot · Last updated: 29 September 2026

snapalerts is a free price-alert bot for Telegram. This policy explains what data the bot collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Your Telegram chat ID — so the bot can send you alerts.
  • Product links you ask the bot to watch, and your alert settings (target price, price-drop, back-in-stock, or page-change alerts).
  • Price history recorded while checking your watched products.
  • If you use /setchannel, the channel name you provide, so alerts can also be posted there.

What we do NOT collect

  • No name, phone number, email address, or other personal details.
  • No passwords or payment information.

How your data is used

Your data is used only to check prices and send you the alerts you asked for. It is never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or third parties.

Data removal

Use /remove in the bot to stop watching a product. To delete everything the bot stores about you, simply stop using the bot and block it — or contact us and we will remove your data.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the bot owner through Telegram at @omtechwatchbot.
